# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

We are committed to a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone. Participants and contributors must be respectful, constructive, and mindful of different backgrounds and levels of experience. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and disruption of workshops or project discussions are not acceptable.
